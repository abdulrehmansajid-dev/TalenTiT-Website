# 100 Department-Wise 15-Minute Training Tasks — Design

## Goal
Replace the homepage **Open Roles** section with a professional, theme-aligned catalogue of 100 department-grouped 15-minute hospitality training tasks.

## Placement
- Same homepage slot as Open Roles (after Gallery Preview, before Testimonials).
- Section id: `training-tasks`.
- Route: `/training-tasks` (replace `/roles`).

## UX
- Department tabs; one active department task list at a time.
- Header: eyebrow, title, short intro copy.
- Stat strip: 100 Topics · 15 Minutes · 14 Departments (+ All Departments as a tab).
- Task rows: serial number + task title (table-like, not card grid).
- Soft fade-in on tab change; navy `#04172f` / orange `#ea580c` accents.

## Content
- Intro: 97 original topics + 3 additional, grouped by department; serials restart per department.
- Departments and tasks as provided by product (100 total).

## Out of scope
- CV / open-roles CTA block.
- Nav link (Open Roles was not in primary nav).
- Backend or PDF download.
